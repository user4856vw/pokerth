# Kibitzer v6 strategic correction (draft experiment)
Base: experiment/kibitzer-evidence-corpus-v5. v4/v5 remain frozen.
 
## Algorithms
- `kibitzer_board_analysis.h`: direct 5–7 card rank evaluation, board pair/trip/suit texture, private made-component vs board-only kicker, immediate straight/flush cards. HU turn: exact 45,540 joint opponent/runout deals; river: exact 990 opponent holdings. On turn each candidate river card reports posterior probability and expected pot share given that card. Equivalent winning draw outs = (46 - 2*N) * sum of the winning draw probability mass (includes split pots). Not the same as the nominal count out of 46 unseen cards.
- Public action trace from local GUI action callbacks: seat, action, board prefix, committed chips, incremental amount, remaining stack and pot after action; bounded per hand. Discard stale/duplicate observations where detectable. Only real bets/raises with positive increment contribute to the provisional likelihood. Earlier same-opponent priced HU decision fills a missing street; never guess earlier multiway aggressor identity. Checks/calls are captured, but not yet weighted. Callback completeness and accurate pot sizing require device validation.
- Weight each surviving opponent combination by product of action likelihoods. Three scenarios (floors .06/.18/.40; strength buckets and bet intensity heuristic) are NOT fitted bluff probabilities or solver strategies. No access to hidden cards. HU preflop/flop uses sampled deals; multiway uses collision-free joint importance sampling, rejecting low effective sample size. Opponents with no evidence remain uniform; folded cards and action dependencies not modeled. Existing manual postflop ranges override automatic inference.
- Preflop flat-call guard: reject material random/conditioned equity deficits on speculative hands and exceptionally large (>5 BB) raises without stronger holdings; AA/short-stack paths preserved. Cheap HU defense allowed before short-stack rules if price <=10% of pot and remaining stack and value-heavy equity exceeds price threshold by 3 points. Covered HU all-in is permitted; multiway all-in and own all-in calls do not receive the conditioned pot-EV overlay. No side-pot-specific EV/ICM/future betting model.
- Postflop: conditional equity guard, extra margin for exposed board-only hands; fallback guard when no reliable range on dangerous texture; preserve made hands and meaningful draws. Unmodeled board-only free value probes are disabled on dangerous boards. No blanket paired-board fold.
- HU position fallback from SB/BB if dealer marker absent. Existing v5 source opening comparison remains shadow-only.
